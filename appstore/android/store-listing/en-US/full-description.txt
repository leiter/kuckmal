Kuckmal brings the media libraries ("Mediatheken") of the German-language public broadcasters together in a single app. Instead of switching between the ARD, ZDF and Arte apps, you search the entire catalogue at once.

ALL BROADCASTERS, ONE APP
More than 400,000 items from the libraries of ARD, ZDF, Arte, 3sat, Phoenix, BR, HR, MDR, NDR, RBB, SR, SWR, WDR, KiKA, ORF, SRF and others – bundled and searchable.

EASY NAVIGATION
• Browse broadcasters: pick a channel and see what it currently offers
• Explore topics: every series of a channel at a glance
• Find titles: jump straight to the episode you are looking for

POWERFUL SEARCH
Full-text search covers titles, topics and descriptions of every available item – across all broadcasters or narrowed to one. A time-period filter hides older material.

DETAILED INFORMATION
Every item shows broadcast date, time, running time and file size before you start it.

PLAY AND DOWNLOAD
Play items directly in the app, or download them in your choice of quality (high/low) for later.

OFFLINE CATALOGUE
The media library index is stored on your device, so browsing and searching stay fast and work without a connection. Playback itself requires internet access.

PRIVACY
• No account, no registration
• No advertising
• No analytics or tracking services
• Your favourites and history stay on the device

ABOUT FIRST-TIME SETUP
On first launch Kuckmal downloads the complete media library index (about 75 MB). Setup takes a few minutes depending on your device and temporarily needs several hundred MB of free storage. Wi-Fi is recommended.

GEO NOTICES
Some items are made available by the broadcasters in certain countries only. Kuckmal points out those restrictions before you start playback.

SUPPORTED BROADCASTERS
ARD (Das Erste), ZDF, Arte, 3sat, Phoenix, BR, HR, MDR, NDR, RBB, SR, SWR, WDR, KiKA, ZDFtivi, funk, ORF, SRF and more.

Kuckmal is an independent project and is not affiliated with any of the broadcasters named above. All content is streamed directly from the official servers of the respective broadcaster; the metadata is based on the freely available film list of the MediathekView project. Kuckmal does not host, circumvent or modify any content.

Note: the content itself is almost entirely in German.
