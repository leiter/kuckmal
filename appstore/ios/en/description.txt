Kuckmal brings the media libraries of the German-language public broadcasters together in a single app. Instead of switching between the ARD, ZDF and Arte apps, you search the entire catalogue with one search.

ALL CHANNELS, ONE APP
More than 400,000 programmes from the media libraries of ARD, ZDF, Arte, 3sat, Phoenix, BR, HR, MDR, NDR, RBB, SR, SWR, WDR, KiKA, ORF, SRF and more — bundled and searchable.

EASY NAVIGATION
• Browse channels: pick a broadcaster and see what it currently offers
• Explore topics: all of a channel's series at a glance
• Find titles: jump straight to the episode you are looking for

POWERFUL SEARCH
Full-text search covers titles, topics and descriptions of every available programme — across all channels or narrowed to one. A time-period filter hides older entries.

DETAILED INFORMATION
For every programme you see the broadcast date, time, running time and file size before you start it.

PLAY AND DOWNLOAD
Programmes are handed to your device's player for playback, or can be downloaded in your choice of quality (high/low) for later. Downloaded files appear in the Files app under "Kuckmal".

OFFLINE CATALOGUE
The media library index is stored on your device, so browsing the catalogue is fast and works without a connection — playback itself requires internet access.

PRIVACY
• No account, no registration
• No advertising
• No analytics or tracking services
• Your favourites and history stay on your device

BEFORE YOU START
On first launch Kuckmal downloads the complete media library index (about 75 MB). Setup takes a few minutes depending on your device and temporarily needs several hundred MB of free space. Wi-Fi is recommended.

GEO-RESTRICTIONS
Some programmes are made available by the broadcasters only in certain countries. Kuckmal flags such restrictions before you start playback. The region used for that hint is read from your device settings and is never transmitted.

SUPPORTED BROADCASTERS
ARD (Das Erste), ZDF, Arte, 3sat, Phoenix, BR, HR, MDR, NDR, RBB, SR, SWR, WDR, KiKA, ZDFtivi, funk, ORF, SRF and more.

Kuckmal is an independent project and is not affiliated with any of the broadcasters named. All content comes directly from the official servers of the respective broadcaster; the metadata is based on the freely available film list of the MediathekView project. Kuckmal hosts, circumvents and modifies no content.

Note: the content itself is almost entirely in German.
