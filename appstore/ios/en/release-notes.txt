First release of Kuckmal.

• Media libraries of ARD, ZDF, Arte, 3sat, Phoenix and many more in one app
• Browse by channel and topic
• Full-text search across titles, topics and descriptions
• Time-period filter for recent programmes
• Detail view with broadcast date, running time and file size
• Playback and download in your choice of quality
• No account, no ads, no tracking

Note: on first launch the media library index is downloaded (about 75 MB). This takes a few minutes — Wi-Fi is recommended.

Enjoy exploring!
