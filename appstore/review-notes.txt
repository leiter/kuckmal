Notes for Apple Review Team
============================

APP OVERVIEW
Kuckmal is an aggregator app for German public broadcasting media libraries
("Mediatheken"). It provides a unified interface to browse and search content
from ARD, ZDF, Arte, 3sat, Phoenix and other German public broadcasters.

NO LOGIN REQUIRED
- The app does not require any user account or login
- All features are accessible immediately upon launch
- No demo account is needed for testing

FIRST LAUNCH — PLEASE READ BEFORE TESTING
On first launch the app downloads the media library index (~75 MB) and unpacks
it. This is a one-time setup that can take several minutes depending on device
and network, and it temporarily needs a few hundred MB of free space. A progress
dialog is shown throughout. The app is not frozen during this step. Wi-Fi is
recommended. Until this completes, browsing lists will be empty.

CONTENT SOURCE
- All content comes from publicly available sources. The programme metadata is
  the freely published film list of the MediathekView project
  (https://liste.mediathekview.de).
- The broadcasters (ARD, ZDF, etc.) are publicly funded institutions comparable
  to the BBC in the UK or PBS/NPR in the US.
- Kuckmal hosts no content, modifies no content, and circumvents no access
  restriction or DRM. It aggregates publicly accessible metadata and hands the
  official stream URL to the system for playback.
- Kuckmal is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
  partnered with any of the broadcasters. The app states this in its store
  description.

HOW TO TEST
1. Launch the app and wait for the one-time index download to finish
2. Browse channels by selecting any broadcaster (e.g. ARD, ZDF)
3. Use the search feature to find specific programmes
4. Tap any item to see detailed information
5. Play — the stream opens in the system player / the broadcaster's player
6. Download — where a downloadable file is offered, it is saved to the app's
   Documents folder and is visible in the Files app under Kuckmal

GEO-RESTRICTION NOTE
Some broadcaster content is geo-restricted to Germany (or the DACH region) by
the broadcasters themselves. If review is performed outside Germany, some items
may not play. This is a restriction imposed by the broadcasters' servers, not by
the app. The app shows an advisory banner for items likely to be affected; the
country used for that hint is read from the device's own region setting and is
never transmitted.

DATA & PRIVACY
- No user account, no registration
- No analytics, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising, no tracking, no IDFA
- No personal data is collected or transmitted to us; we operate no backend
- Settings, favourites, history, the media index and downloads are stored only
  in app-private storage on the device
- Outbound network connections are limited to the MediathekView film list and
  the broadcasters' own streaming servers, which necessarily see the user's IP
  as with any internet connection

LANGUAGE
- The app interface is localized in German and English and follows the device
  language setting
- The programme content itself is almost entirely in German, as it comes from
  German broadcasters

AGE RATING
The rating reflects the broadcasters' unfiltered catalogue, which includes crime
drama, news footage of war and disasters, and documentaries on crime and drug
use. Kuckmal does not filter or modify this content and provides no parental
controls, so the rating was answered against the catalogue as a whole rather
than against the app's own interface.
